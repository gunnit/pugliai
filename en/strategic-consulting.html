Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

How does AI strategic consulting for SMBs work?
Our AI strategic consulting begins with a complete assessment of business processes, identifying the highest-impact opportunities. We then develop a personalized roadmap with priorities, timelines and detailed budgets, supporting you in implementation with weekly or monthly sessions for 3-12 months.
How long does an AI consulting project typically last?
AI strategic consulting projects typically last 3-12 months, depending on objectives. The assessment phase takes 2-3 weeks, followed by strategy and roadmap (1-2 weeks) and then implementation and continuous support. Each project is structured with clear milestones and measurable results.
What results can I expect from AI strategic consulting?
SMBs following our AI strategic consulting achieve on average: +30% operational efficiency, -25% operational costs, +40% decision-making speed and an average 200% ROI within 18 months. We always provide concrete and measurable metrics for every intervention.
Does consulting include technical support for implementation?
Yes, strategic consulting includes complete technical support: vendor selection, implementation supervision, team training, change management and continuous monitoring. We never leave you alone with the strategy, but accompany you to concrete results.
Is technical experience necessary to benefit from AI consulting?
Absolutely not. Our consulting is designed for CEOs and leadership teams without technical background. We translate all AI concepts into concrete business terms, focus on ROI and measurable results, also providing basic training to the team to ensure future autonomy.
