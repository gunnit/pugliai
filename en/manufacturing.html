Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI improve manufacturing efficiency?

AI transforms manufacturing through predictive maintenance (reducing downtime by 30%), computer vision quality control (detecting 90% more defects), and supply chain optimization (improving efficiency by 25%). These Industry 4.0 solutions deliver measurable ROI within the first months of implementation.

What is predictive maintenance and how does it work?

Predictive maintenance uses AI to analyze data from IoT sensors on your machinery to anticipate failures before they occur. By monitoring vibration, temperature, pressure, and other signals, our models predict when equipment needs servicing, reducing unplanned downtime by up to 30% and cutting maintenance costs significantly.

Can AI integrate with existing factory systems like MES, ERP, and SCADA?

Yes. PugliAI specializes in IT/OT integration, connecting AI solutions seamlessly with your existing MES, SCADA, PLC, and ERP systems. We bridge the gap between operational technology and information technology, ensuring AI models work within your current infrastructure without disruption.

What is a digital twin and how can it help my factory?

A digital twin is a virtual replica of your production processes that allows you to simulate, test, and validate AI solutions in a virtual environment before real implementation. This reduces risk, accelerates deployment, and enables continuous optimization of your manufacturing operations.

How long does it take to implement AI in a manufacturing plant?
