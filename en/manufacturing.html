Our Method for the Smart Factory

An engineering approach in 4 phases to digitize your production processes.

1

Plant and Data Analysis

We start by analyzing your facilities, production flows, and available data (MES, ERP, SCADA) to identify the highest potential use cases.

2

Digital Twin Development

We create a 'digital twin' of your processes to simulate, test, and validate AI solutions in a virtual environment before real implementation.

3

On-Premise / Edge Integration

We deploy AI models directly on your systems, ensuring real-time performance, data security, and integration with OT/IT infrastructure.

4

Monitoring and Continuous Improvement

We monitor key KPIs (OEE, TEEP, MTBF) and continuously train models to adapt to new conditions and maximize performance over time.
