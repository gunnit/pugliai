Everything you want to know

What exactly does an AI package include?
Each package includes everything needed for your AI transformation: continuous strategic consulting for 3–12 months, pre-built automations for marketing and sales ready to use, complete team training, implementation of customized solutions and dedicated support. There are no hidden costs or additional services to purchase.
Why choose a complete package instead of individual services?
With a fragmented approach, many SMEs fail in implementation. Our integrated method combines strategy, technology, training and support to ensure concrete, measurable results. The total cost is 40% lower than purchasing separate services.
How long does it take to see the first results?
With our pre-built automations, you'll see first results within 2–4 weeks of launch. Within 90 days you'll have implemented the first set of solutions and your team will be operational. Full ROI typically manifests within 6–12 months.
Is my team ready for AI? We don't have technical skills.
That's exactly why our packages include complete training: no prior technical skills needed. Our solutions are user-friendly and we include practical workshops, certifications and continuous support. 95% of our clients started with no AI experience and now manage the solutions independently.
Can I start with the Accelerator package and then scale up?
Absolutely. Many clients start with AI Accelerator (€15K–25K) to validate the approach and then upgrade to Transformation or Partnership. 70% of the initial investment is credited toward the next package: a gradual path that reduces risk.
Is there an ROI guarantee?
Yes, ROI is contractually guaranteed on all packages: 150% in the first year for AI Accelerator, 200% for AI Transformation and 250%+ for AI Partnership. Zero hidden costs and flexible payments.
