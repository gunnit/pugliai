Why On-Premise

Your data under your control

In a world where data is the new oil, keeping it under your control isn't a luxury, it's a necessity.

Complete data sovereignty

Your customers' voice conversations stay on your servers. No external cloud, no risk of data breach, no dependency on third-party providers.

Zero latency

Local processing means instant responses. No delay from round-trips to external clouds. Fluid and natural conversations like with a human operator.

Native GDPR compliance

Ready for Privacy Authority inspections. Complete audit trail, configurable data retention, guaranteed deletion. No data transfer outside the EU.

Predictable costs

One-time investment plus fixed maintenance. No variable cost per minute or per call. The more you use the system, the more you save compared to cloud.

Always works

No internet dependency for core functionality. The system works even offline or with limited connectivity. Business continuity guaranteed.

No vendor lock-in

The infrastructure is your property. No dependency on cloud vendors who can change prices or terms. Complete freedom.

Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What does VoiceAI On-Premise mean?
VoiceAI On-Premise is an AI voice assistant installed directly on your company's servers. Voice data is never sent to external clouds: everything stays under your control, ensuring maximum privacy and GDPR compliance.
What are the advantages over cloud solutions?
The main advantages are: complete data sovereignty, zero latency for instant responses, native GDPR compliance, no variable usage-based costs, operation even without an internet connection and full control over the infrastructure.
How much does VoiceAI On-Premise cost?
Packages start from €25,000 for the Starter version (1 line, business hours), €45,000 for Business (5 lines, 24/7, multilingual), up to €75,000 and above for Enterprise with unlimited lines and advanced customizations.
What hardware requirements are needed?
For a typical SME you need: a server with a GPU (NVIDIA RTX 4090 or higher recommended), 64–128GB RAM, 1TB SSD. We can provide the server already configured as a turnkey option, or install on your existing systems.
Does it integrate with our existing switchboard?
Yes, VoiceAI On-Premise integrates with the main business phone systems (traditional PBX, VoIP, SIP trunk), CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho) and Italian ERP systems (Zucchetti, TeamSystem, SAP). Integration is included in the package.
