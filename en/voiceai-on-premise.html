Why On-Premise?

In a world where data is the new oil, keeping it under your control isn't a luxury - it's a necessity.

Complete Data Sovereignty

Your customers' voice conversations stay on your servers. No external cloud, no risk of data breach, no dependency on third-party providers.

Zero Latency

Local processing = instant responses. No delay from round-trips to external clouds. Fluid and natural conversations like with a human operator.

Native GDPR Compliance

Ready for Privacy Authority inspections. Complete audit trail, configurable data retention, guaranteed deletion. No data transfer outside EU.

Predictable Costs

One-time investment + fixed maintenance. No variable cost per minute or per call. The more you use the system, the more you save compared to cloud.

Always Works

No internet dependency for core functionality. The system works even offline or with limited connectivity. Business continuity guaranteed.

No Vendor Lock-in

The infrastructure is your property. No dependency on cloud vendors who can change prices or terms. Complete freedom.
