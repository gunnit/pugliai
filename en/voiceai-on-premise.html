Why on-premise

Six reasons to keep customers’ voices in the company.

Data sovereignty

Conversations stay on your servers: no external cloud, no transfers outside the European Union.

Minimal latency

Local processing: immediate answers and conversations as smooth as with an operator.

GDPR compliance

Activity log, configurable retention, guaranteed deletion. Ready for regulator audits.

Predictable costs

Upfront investment and fixed maintenance: no cost per minute or per call.

Always on

Core functions do not depend on the internet: business continuity even with limited connectivity.

No vendor lock-in

The infrastructure is yours: no dependence on vendors who can change prices and terms.

Frequently asked questions

How much does VoiceAI On-Premise cost?

From €25,000 (Starter, one line, office hours) to €45,000 (Business, five lines, 24/7) up to over €75,000 for the Enterprise version with unlimited lines and server included. Prices exclude VAT.

How does it integrate with my switchboard?

Through SIP trunk, PBX or VoIP. During installation we configure routing, hours and the cases in which to hand the call to an operator.

How long does activation take?

From order to production it takes 4–6 weeks on average, including CRM and ERP integrations and team training.

Does voice data really stay in the company?

Yes. Speech recognition, synthesis and transcription run on the server installed on site or in a private cloud you control. No audio is sent to external services.
