Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What exactly is the AI POC Framework?
The POC Framework is a structured 4-phase methodology to test AI solutions in 30 days with zero risk. It includes initial assessment, solution design, test implementation, and ROI validation. With a 95% success rate, it allows SMEs to validate AI before making the full investment.
How much does a POC Framework cost and what does it include?
The POC Framework has a fixed cost of €5,000-15,000 depending on complexity. It includes: comprehensive assessment, working prototype development, testing with real data, detailed report with projected ROI, and implementation plan. Total refund guarantee if the POC doesn't meet agreed objectives.
What happens if the POC Framework doesn't work?
We offer a total refund guarantee if the POC doesn't meet the contractually agreed objectives. In 5 years, we have refunded only 5% of projects. If the POC succeeds, the investment is fully deducted from the complete implementation, making the test virtually free.
Can I continue with implementation if the POC succeeds?
Absolutely yes. The POC Framework is designed for continuity: if the test succeeds, we move directly to full implementation, scaling all the work already done. The POC cost is completely deducted from the total investment, ensuring a smooth transition from proof to production.
Is the POC Framework suitable for my SME?
The POC Framework is ideal for SMEs with 15 to 500 employees who want to test AI without risks. It's perfect if you have repetitive processes, manage large volumes of data or documents, or want to automate customer service, sales, or analytics. A brief consultation allows us to assess suitability within 24 hours.
