Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What kind of AI case studies does PugliAI showcase?
PugliAI showcases real AI transformation case studies across multiple industries including manufacturing, finance, retail, healthcare and logistics. Each case study documents the complete journey from strategy to implementation, with measurable results such as ROI figures, efficiency gains and cost savings achieved by Italian SMEs.
What results can I expect from AI implementation?
Results vary by industry and use case, but PugliAI's portfolio demonstrates consistent outcomes: across 47 completed projects, clients have achieved an average 3.2x ROI, generated over €12M in value and reached time-to-value in an average of 8 weeks. Specific examples include 32% waste reduction in manufacturing, 75% faster credit approvals in finance and 45% higher average order value in retail.
How long does an AI implementation project take?
PugliAI's AI implementation projects typically achieve measurable results within 6 to 12 weeks. The average time-to-value across all case studies is 8 weeks. For example, Gruppo Tessile Lombardo saw results in 6 weeks, while more complex projects like Banca Popolare del Nord's credit analysis automation were completed in 10 weeks.
Which industries benefit most from AI transformation?
PugliAI has delivered successful AI projects across manufacturing (predictive maintenance, quality control), finance (credit analysis, KYC automation), retail (demand forecasting, personalization), healthcare (diagnostic support, workflow optimization) and logistics (route optimization, predictive maintenance). Manufacturing and finance have shown particularly strong ROI, with savings up to €4.2M per year.
How does PugliAI measure the ROI of AI projects?
PugliAI measures ROI through concrete, documented metrics specific to each project. These include cost savings (e.g. €2.1M annual savings for Gruppo Tessile Lombardo), efficiency improvements (e.g. +24% production efficiency), error reduction (e.g. 99.2% accuracy in credit analysis) and time savings (e.g. 75% reduction in approval time). Each case study includes verified before-and-after data with clear KPIs.
