Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

How do AI agents for SMBs work?
AI agents are intelligent systems that automate specific processes: customer service chatbots, sales automation, document analysis and 24/7 support. They use technologies like NLP and machine learning to understand and respond to requests naturally, reducing operational costs by 60%.
How long does it take to implement an AI agent?
Implementation typically takes 2–6 weeks depending on complexity. We start with a basic chatbot in 2 weeks, then gradually add advanced features. Team training happens in parallel to ensure rapid adoption.
Do AI agents replace employees?
No, AI agents empower existing teams by automating repetitive and routine tasks. Employees focus on value-added activities, strategic customer relationships and complex decisions. The goal is to increase productivity, not replace people.
What results can I expect from an AI agent?
SMBs implementing our AI agents see on average: +40% in conversions, −60% in support costs, +35% in customer satisfaction and −80% in response times. Results are measurable from the first month of use.
Are AI agents secure for business data?
Absolutely. All our AI agents are GDPR compliant, ISO 27001 certified and comply with the European AI Act. Data always remains under company control, with end-to-end encryption and hosting in Europe to ensure complete privacy and compliance.
