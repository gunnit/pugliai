Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI package is best for an SME just starting out?

For SMEs starting with AI we recommend the AI Accelerator package, €15,000-25,000. It includes 3 months of consulting, pre-built automations and team training. It is the ideal entry point, with a 150% ROI guarantee.

What does the AI Accelerator package include?

The AI Accelerator package includes: a free AI audit, 3 custom automations, 3 months of strategic consulting, training for 10 employees, dedicated technical support and an ROI monitoring dashboard.

How does the 150% ROI guarantee work?

The ROI guarantee is contractual: together we define measurable KPIs (time saved, cost reduction, sales increase). If you do not reach 150% ROI within 12 months, we keep working at no extra cost until the target is met.

Are your services suitable for an SME with no AI experience?

Yes. The AI Accelerator package is designed precisely for SMEs starting from zero: team training, pre-built automations and hands-on support. No prior technical skills are needed — we handle the technical complexity.

Which industries do you serve with your AI packages?

We serve SMEs in every sector: manufacturing, fashion and luxury, financial services, healthcare, tourism, agrifood, retail and professional firms. Each package is tailored to the specific needs of your industry.
