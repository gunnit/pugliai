Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

Which AI package is best for an SME just starting out?
For SMEs starting with AI we recommend the AI Accelerator package (€15,000–25,000). It includes 3 months of consulting, pre-built automations and team training. It's the ideal starting point, with a guaranteed 150% ROI.
What does the AI Accelerator package include?
A free AI audit, 3 custom automations, 3 months of strategic consulting, training for up to 10 people, dedicated technical support and an ROI monitoring dashboard.
How does the 150% ROI guarantee work?
The guarantee is contractual: we define measurable KPIs together (time saved, cost reduction, sales growth). If you don't reach 150% ROI within 12 months, we keep working at no extra cost until you do.
Which industries do your AI packages serve?
We serve SMEs across every sector: manufacturing, fashion and luxury, financial services, healthcare, tourism, food and agriculture, retail and professional firms. Each package is tailored to the sector's needs.
