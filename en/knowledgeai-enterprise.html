How it works

The RAG technology, step by step

RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) combines semantic search in your documents with AI generation for accurate and verifiable responses.

1
Indexing

Document indexing

Your documents (PDF, Word, email, database) are processed and indexed in a local vector database. Each text fragment becomes semantically searchable.

2
Search

Semantic search

When you ask a question, the system searches your documents for the most relevant passages, even if they don't contain the exact words from your question.

3
Generation

Context-aware generation

The AI model generates the response using only the information found in your documents. Every statement is traceable to the original source.

4
Answer

Response with citations

You receive the complete response with links to source documents. You can verify every piece of information by clicking on the reference.

Generic ChatGPT

Invents plausible information, doesn't know your documents, sends data to the cloud and offers no verifiable source.

KnowledgeAI RAG

Answers only from real sources, uses your company documents, processes everything on-premise and backs every response with a citation.

The result

Reliable, traceable answers, a 70-90% reduction in hallucinations versus a generic assistant and data that always stays under your control.

Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What is KnowledgeAI Enterprise?
KnowledgeAI Enterprise is an enterprise AI chat that uses RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) technology to answer employee questions using the company's internal documents. Everything is processed on-premise, without sending data to the cloud.
What does RAG mean and why does it reduce hallucinations?
RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) is a technology that combines document search with AI generation. Before answering, the system searches your documents for the relevant information, then generates a response based on verifiable sources. This reduces hallucinations (invented answers) by 70-90% compared to generic ChatGPT.
Which types of documents does it support?
KnowledgeAI supports PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, email, web pages, SQL databases, and can connect to SharePoint, Google Drive, Confluence and other document repositories. It also supports scanned documents thanks to built-in OCR.
How does access control work?
KnowledgeAI Enterprise includes a Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) system. Each user sees only the documents they are authorized to access. Permissions sync with Active Directory, LDAP or your existing IAM system.
