Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

How long is the program?
The program lasts 16 weeks (4 months), divided into 4 phases: Foundation (weeks 1-4), Build (5-10), Scale (11-14) and Launch (15-16). Each phase has specific goals to take your startup from concept to market.
What are the eligibility requirements?
We look for teams of 1-5 people with an AI/tech-enabled product or service. You must be willing to register (or already have) an Italian entity, have at least one full-time founder, and have a prototype or MVP, even at an early stage.
How much does it cost to participate?
We don't have a fixed model. We evaluate each project individually based on stage, team, market and needs. Terms (equity, fee, revenue share or a combination) are discussed during the selection process.
Do I need to relocate to Brindisi or Bergamo?
No, the program is hybrid. Mentorship sessions are primarily virtual, with some optional in-person events (kickoff, Demo Day). We offer access to PugliAI offices for those who want to work on-site.
What is the Singapore Bridge?
Through our partnership with ISSA (Italian-Singapore Strategic Alliance), we offer selected startups access to over 500 Asian investors, Singapore incorporation support, office space and a "Soft Landing" week for the top 3 startups of each cohort.
When does the next cohort start?
Applications are open for 2026, with 2 cohorts per year (spring and fall). We select 8-10 startups per cohort, so we can offer weekly 1:1 mentorship and personalized attention to each team.
What happens after the program?
After Demo Day, startups join our Alumni Network with permanent access to the AI Founders Italia community, mentorship opportunities for future cohorts, and ongoing support for fundraising and international expansion.
