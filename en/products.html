Frequently Asked Questions

What are on-premise AI products?

On-premise AI products are artificial intelligence solutions installed directly on your company's infrastructure, rather than running in the cloud. This means your data never leaves your servers, ensuring maximum privacy, full GDPR compliance, and complete data sovereignty. PugliAI offers four on-premise products: VoiceAI for intelligent voice assistance, KnowledgeAI for enterprise document chat, MCP Hosting for securely connecting AI to business systems, and Autonomous AI Agents that execute tasks across those systems.

Why choose on-premise AI over cloud solutions?

On-premise AI offers several advantages for SMBs: your sensitive data stays within your company walls ensuring native GDPR compliance, you maintain total control over your AI systems, there is zero latency since processing happens locally, and you avoid ongoing cloud subscription costs. This is especially critical for industries handling confidential customer data, financial records, or proprietary manufacturing processes.
