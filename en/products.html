Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What are on-premise AI products?
On-premise AI products are artificial intelligence solutions installed directly on your company's infrastructure, rather than running in the cloud. This means your data never leaves your servers, ensuring maximum privacy, full GDPR compliance and complete data sovereignty. PugliAI offers two on-premise products: VoiceAI for intelligent voice assistance and KnowledgeAI for enterprise document chat.
Why choose on-premise AI over cloud solutions?
On-premise AI offers several advantages for SMBs: your sensitive data stays within your company walls ensuring native GDPR compliance, you keep total control over your AI systems, there is zero latency since processing happens locally, and you avoid ongoing cloud subscription costs. This is especially critical for industries handling confidential customer data, financial records or proprietary manufacturing processes.
What products does PugliAI offer?
PugliAI offers two enterprise on-premise AI products designed for Italian SMBs. VoiceAI On-Premise is an AI voice assistant for intelligent switchboard and automated customer service with 24/7 availability and CRM/ERP integration, priced from €25,000 to €75,000. KnowledgeAI Enterprise is an AI-powered chat system using RAG technology to query internal documents like PDFs, Word, Excel and emails with a 70-90% reduction in AI hallucinations, priced from €30,000 to €85,000.
How much do on-premise AI solutions cost?
PugliAI's on-premise AI solutions are competitively priced for SMBs. VoiceAI On-Premise ranges from €25,000 to €75,000 depending on the scope of integration and number of channels. KnowledgeAI Enterprise ranges from €30,000 to €85,000 based on document volume and complexity. Both products include installation, configuration and integration with your existing systems like CRM, ERP and ticketing platforms.
Can on-premise AI products integrate with existing systems?
Yes, both PugliAI products are designed to integrate seamlessly with your existing business systems. VoiceAI On-Premise connects with CRM, ERP and ticketing systems for intelligent call routing and customer service automation. KnowledgeAI Enterprise supports multiple document formats including PDF, Word, Excel and email, and maintains an always up-to-date knowledge base from your internal documents. Both solutions are tailored to fit your specific infrastructure.
