Frequently Asked Questions

What are on-premise AI products?

On-premise AI products are artificial intelligence solutions installed directly on your company's infrastructure, rather than running in the cloud. This means your data never leaves your servers, ensuring maximum privacy, full GDPR compliance, and complete data sovereignty. PugliAI offers two on-premise products: VoiceAI for intelligent voice assistance and KnowledgeAI for enterprise document chat.

Why choose on-premise AI over cloud solutions?

On-premise AI offers several advantages for SMBs: your sensitive data stays within your company walls ensuring native GDPR compliance, you maintain total control over your AI systems, there is zero latency since processing happens locally, and you avoid ongoing cloud subscription costs. This is especially critical for industries handling confidential customer data, financial records, or proprietary manufacturing processes.

What products does PugliAI offer?

PugliAI offers two enterprise on-premise AI products designed for Italian SMBs. VoiceAI On-Premise is an AI voice assistant for intelligent switchboard and automated customer service with 24/7 availability and CRM/ERP integration, priced from EUR 25,000 to EUR 75,000.
