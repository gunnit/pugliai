Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What kind of AI training does PugliAI offer?
PugliAI offers a complete training program that includes practical guides, strategic whitepapers, expert webinars, executive courses and certified programs. The Academy has over 150 resources and three main paths: the AI Leadership Track for C-level and senior management, the AI Engineering Path for developers and data scientists, and customized Corporate Training for company teams.
How long does the AI training last?
The duration depends on the chosen path. The AI Leadership Track consists of 3 modules of 2 days each. The AI Engineering Path lasts 8 weeks part-time. The AI Leadership Masterclass is an intensive 3-day course. Corporate Training programs have a customized duration based on the company's needs.
Is the training suitable for people without technical skills?
Yes, absolutely. The AI Leadership Track is designed specifically for C-level and senior management without a technical background, focusing on strategy, governance and change management. Corporate Training can be tailored to any skill level, from beginners to experts, with teams of 10 to 100 people.
What certifications do you receive?
All main training paths include a final certification issued by PugliAI Academy. The AI Leadership Track includes a certification on the AI strategy framework. The AI Engineering Path includes access to professional tools and a technical certification. With over 2,500 executives already trained, PugliAI certifications are recognized in the Italian AI sector.
How is the training delivered?
Training is delivered flexibly: Corporate Training programs can be run on-site at your company or remotely. The paths include industry case studies, 1-on-1 coaching sessions, cloud practice labs, real-world projects and ongoing mentorship. On-demand resources such as guides, whitepapers and recorded webinars are also available.
