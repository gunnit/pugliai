Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

How does AI help detect financial fraud?
AI analyzes millions of data points in milliseconds to identify suspicious transactions and fraud patterns in real-time. Our machine learning models use behavioral analysis to detect anomalies, reducing false positives by 70% while catching fraud that traditional rule-based systems miss.
Is AI compliant with financial regulations like GDPR, PSD2, and MiFID II?
Yes. Data security and regulatory compliance are the foundation of every solution we design. We build compliance by design into all AI systems, meeting GDPR, PSD2, MiFID II, and KYC/AML requirements. We also use Explainable AI (XAI) to make algorithmic decisions transparent and auditable.
What results can financial institutions expect from AI?
Our financial AI solutions deliver measurable results: -70% reduction in false positive fraud detection, +95% accuracy in credit scoring, -80% document processing time, and significant improvements in customer satisfaction through personalized advisory services.
How does PugliAI's robo-advisory solution work?
Our robo-advisory platforms combine AI-driven portfolio management with personalized investment strategies. They can be fully automated or hybrid (augmenting human advisors), making professional financial advice accessible to a wider audience while maintaining regulatory compliance.
Can AI integrate with existing core banking systems?
Absolutely. We integrate AI solutions into your existing core banking, trading systems, and other financial infrastructure with a focus on security, privacy, and business continuity. Our rigorous 4-phase framework ensures seamless integration with backtesting and validation before deployment.
