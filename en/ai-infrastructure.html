Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What is AI infrastructure and why do SMEs need it?
AI infrastructure is the technical foundation (cloud platforms, data pipelines, MLOps tools and security layers) that enables businesses to run AI workloads reliably. SMEs need it to deploy AI models at scale, process data securely and maintain 99.9% uptime without building expensive in-house systems.
How long does it take to set up AI infrastructure?
PugliAI completes AI infrastructure setup in as little as 15 days for starter packages. Our proven methodology covers assessment, architecture design, cloud configuration, data pipeline implementation and team training. Full enterprise setups typically take 60-90 days.
How much does AI infrastructure cost compared to on-premise?
Our cloud-native AI infrastructure reduces costs by up to 70% compared to traditional on-premise setups. Packages start at €15,000 for starter infrastructure, €35,000 for professional and €75,000 for enterprise-grade. You pay only for what you use, with auto-scaling capabilities.
Which cloud providers does PugliAI support?
We build multi-provider cloud-native platforms across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud Platform. Our infrastructure uses Kubernetes for orchestration, Docker for containerization and Terraform for infrastructure-as-code, ensuring flexibility and avoiding vendor lock-in.
Is PugliAI's AI infrastructure GDPR compliant?
Yes, all our infrastructure is GDPR compliant by design. We implement end-to-end encryption, strict access controls, comprehensive audit trails and data residency in EU regions. Our security framework also supports HIPAA, PCI-DSS and ISO 27001 requirements.
