Our continuous growth

Since 2023, PugliAI has transformed its vision into concrete reality. With over 50 SMEs transformed and a constantly growing team, we have become the reference point for SME AI in Italy.

Acceleration and synergy: the partnership with Feedel Ventures

Our growth found a strategic catalyst in Feedel Ventures. This alliance has allowed us to enter an innovation ecosystem, supporting the most visionary startups in their portfolio and refining our solutions through contact with real market challenges.

Success story: revolutionizing real estate with Hausme

With Hausme, we demonstrated the transformative potential of AI. By developing custom intelligent agents, we helped this promising startup optimize operations, personalize the user experience, and attack the market with unprecedented innovative force.

Social impact: Get Your Grant is born

From PugliAI's experience came Get Your Grant, a spinoff that embodies our mission to use technology for the common good. This AI-powered platform simplifies access to Erasmus funds, opening a world of opportunities for youth and organizations.

Process innovation: partnership with OpenWork (Jamio)

The alliance with OpenWork and its Jamio BPM platform marks a quality leap in process digitalization. By integrating our AI agents into their low-code/no-code Business Process Management software, we orchestrate and automate the most complex workflows, turning business processes into intelligent strategic assets.

Winners of the Comtel Startup Challenge

The recognition obtained at the Comtel Startup Challenge confirms the value of our vision. We are now collaborating with Comtel to develop innovative solutions that redefine the future of telecommunications through artificial intelligence.

Exclusive distributors of Automa for Italy

We are proud to be the exclusive distributors for Italy of Automa, the cutting-edge RPA platform that combines robotic automation and artificial intelligence. This partnership allows us to offer our clients unprecedented intelligent automation solutions.
