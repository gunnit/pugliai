Our story

From idea to impact

A journey through innovation, strategic partnerships and the successes that define who we are. Since 2023, PugliAI has become the reference point for SME AI in Italy.

December 2023 — Foundation

PugliAI is born with the vision to democratize AI for Italian businesses.

February 2024 — Feedel Ventures

First strategic partnership: the agreement with Feedel Ventures accelerates our growth and opens an innovation ecosystem.

April 2024 — 50th SME

We reach the milestone of the first 50 SMEs transformed.

June 2024 — Get Your Grant

Launch of the spinoff that democratizes access to Erasmus funding with AI.

October 2024 — Comtel Challenge

Winners of the Comtel Startup Challenge, recognized for innovation in telecommunications.

December 2024 — Automa Italy

We become exclusive distributors of Automa for Italy, combining robotic automation and AI.

Partnerships and success stories

Alliances that create value

Growing through contact with real market challenges, refining our solutions alongside visionary partners.
