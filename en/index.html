Before we begin

Let’s make
things clear.

Where do we start?+

With a free 45-minute strategy session. Tell us about a repetitive task and the tools you use: we assess the opportunities and prepare a report with an estimate of the potential return.

Do we need an in-house technical team?+

It is not a requirement. We support you in assessing your systems, integrating the solution and training the people who will use it.

Can our data stay within the company?+

Yes. On-premise solutions are installed on your infrastructure. We review servers, access permissions and project requirements together before defining the setup.

How are costs and timelines agreed?+

We start with the project scope and agree on the work, timeline and measures of success. Consulting programmes have a price agreed before work begins. The first session also helps us identify the right programme.
