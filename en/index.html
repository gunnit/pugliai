Answers to Your Questions about AI for SMEs

Everything you need to know about our AI packages, implementation, costs and expected results

How long does AI package implementation take?

Standard implementation takes 90 days from contract signing. This includes initial assessment (2 weeks), development and configuration (6-8 weeks), testing and training (2-3 weeks), and go-live with intensive support (1 week). For urgent projects, we offer a 45-day accelerated path with dedicated team.

What are the minimum technical requirements to start?

No complex technical prerequisites are required. You only need: stable internet connection, access to existing business systems (ERP, CRM if present), team availability for training. Our cloud infrastructure handles everything else. No need to purchase additional hardware or software.

How do you calculate the guaranteed 150% ROI?

ROI is calculated on concrete and measurable metrics: operating cost reduction (20-40%), productivity increase (25-35%), error reduction (60-80%), time freed for strategic activities (30%). We document every metric before and after implementation. If we don't reach promised ROI, we continue working for free until objectives are achieved.

Will AI replace our employees?

Absolutely not. AI empowers your team, doesn't replace it. It automates repetitive tasks allowing employees to focus on higher value-added activities. 95% of our implementations have led to staff growth thanks to business increase, not reductions.

What happens after the first 90 days?

After go-live, support continues according to chosen package: Starter (3 months), Professional (6 months), Enterprise (12 months). Includes proactive monitoring, continuous optimizations, additional training on request, technology updates, priority support. After the included period, we offer customized continuous support plans.

How do you protect our business data?

Security and privacy are absolute priorities. We use end-to-end encryption, hosting in European data centers (GDPR compliant), redundant automatic backups, complete audit logs, ISO 27001 certifications. Your data always remains your property. We sign NDAs and confidentiality agreements before every project.

Can we start with a pilot project?

Certainly! We offer a POC (Proof of Concept) for 30 days at €5,000 (deductible from complete package). Includes implementation of a specific use case, measurable results in 30 days, detailed report with complete roadmap. It's the perfect way to experience AI benefits before full investment.

Which sectors have the best results with AI?

All sectors benefit from AI, but we see exceptional results in: manufacturing (efficiency +40%), retail (sales +30%), financial services (processing time -75%), logistics (route optimization +35%), private healthcare (waiting time reduction -50%). The key is to customize AI for specific needs of each sector and company.
