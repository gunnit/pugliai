Regulation & ethics

Compliance is a competitive advantage

The European and Italian regulatory framework imposes stringent requirements, but it also provides legal certainty for long-term investment. The EU AI Act phases in over time: prohibitions and AI literacy from February 2025, rules for generative models from August 2025, and full compliance for high-risk systems by August 2027.

Italian Law n.1146/2025 adds €1 billion in public AI investment, a hosting requirement on national servers for public administration, the right of workers to be informed about AI use, and copyright protection for works with significant human contribution. For CEOs, the practical playbook is consistent: run an impact assessment and classify every system by risk, keep detailed technical documentation, ensure human oversight of critical decisions, and be transparent with users and employees whenever they interact with AI.

Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

What does the CEO AI Guide cover?
The PugliAI CEO AI Guide is a comprehensive resource covering AI implementation strategies for business leaders. It includes an AI readiness checklist, an ROI calculator, 50+ best practices for AI adoption, guidance on EU AI Act compliance, change management frameworks, and sector-specific strategies for manufacturing, finance, retail and healthcare. The guide provides actionable steps from initial assessment through full-scale deployment.
Who is the guide for?
The guide is designed for CEOs, founders and senior executives of small and medium enterprises who want to understand and leverage artificial intelligence for business growth. It is written in accessible language without excessive technical jargon, making it suitable for leaders who need to make strategic decisions about AI investments without a deep technical background.
How can AI improve my business ROI?
AI can deliver measurable ROI across multiple dimensions: up to 30% reduction in operating costs, 40% increase in productivity, 25% improvement in customer satisfaction and 2x faster time-to-market. The guide includes a detailed ROI calculator and real-world examples showing how Italian SMEs have achieved 150% average ROI within 12 months across manufacturing, finance, retail and other sectors.
What is the AI Act and how does it affect my company?
The EU AI Act is Europe's comprehensive regulatory framework for artificial intelligence, affecting all companies operating in the EU. The guide explains the risk-based classification system (minimal, limited, high and unacceptable risk), the compliance requirements for each category, and the practical steps CEOs should take to ensure their AI implementations meet regulatory standards. It covers GDPR alignment, transparency obligations and governance best practices.
How do I get started with AI for my SME?
The guide outlines a 7-step action plan: assess your company's AI readiness using the included checklist, identify high-impact use cases specific to your industry, start with a focused pilot project to demonstrate value, invest in training and upskilling your team, establish clear AI governance with ethics oversight, collaborate with ecosystem partners including universities and technology providers, and monitor KPIs to iterate continuously. PugliAI offers free consultations to help you begin this journey.
