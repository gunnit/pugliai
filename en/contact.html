Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the free AI strategic session cost?

The AI strategic session is completely free and lasts 45-60 minutes. It includes a preliminary assessment, analysis of specific AI opportunities for your sector, indicative ROI estimate, and high-level roadmap. No purchase obligation, it's our investment to let you discover AI's potential for your SME.

How quickly will I receive a response to my message?

We respond to all requests within 2 business hours (Mon-Fri 9:00-18:00 CET/CEST), often much sooner. For urgent requests, leave your phone number in the form and we will call you back the same day. Requests sent at the weekend are answered on Monday morning.

Is the consulting suitable for my specific SME?

We work with SMEs across all sectors from 10 to 500 employees: manufacturing, retail, services, healthcare, logistics, fintech. Each consultation is customized for sector, size, and specific objectives. During the first call, we assess together the suitability and most promising opportunities for your case.

What should I prepare for the strategic session?

Bring: overview of main processes, key operational challenges, growth objectives, indicative innovation budget, and information about any digital tools already in use. No detailed technical data needed, focus on business and operations.

What happens after the free session?

You'll receive a written report with priority AI opportunities, preliminary ROI estimates, and recommended next steps. If you decide to proceed, we'll design together the most suitable path: POC Framework, complete packages, or strategic consulting. Zero sales pressure, only value and total transparency.
