What is the Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI models connect to tools, data and business applications in a uniform, secure way. Instead of building a bespoke integration for every system, an MCP server exposes the functions of an application — for example the ERP, the CRM or a document archive — in a controlled manner, so an AI model or autonomous agent can read and use them through a single standard interface.

MCP hosting is the service through which PugliAI installs, configures and manages these MCP servers on your infrastructure. The result: your AI becomes genuinely useful because it "sees" the company's real data, yet that data never leaves your systems. It is the infrastructure layer that makes autonomous AI agents and solutions like KnowledgeAI Enterprise safe and reliable.

In short: the MCP server is a secure bridge between AI models and your systems. On-premise MCP hosting puts that bridge under your control, with granular permissions and full tracking of every access.

MCP hosting frequently asked questions

What is the Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI models connect to tools, data and business applications in a uniform, secure way. An MCP server exposes the functions of a system (ERP, CRM, document archive) in a controlled manner, so an AI model or autonomous agent can use them without a bespoke integration for every single tool.

What does on-premise MCP hosting mean?

It means the MCP servers are installed and managed on your own infrastructure (company servers or a private cloud you control), not on external services. This way the data the AI accesses never leaves your company: PugliAI handles installation, configuration, updates and monitoring, while you keep full control and GDPR compliance.

How much does PugliAI's MCP hosting cost?

The service starts at €290/month for the Starter plan (one managed MCP server and up to two standard connectors), €590/month for the Business plan (up to five connectors, SSO, audit log and SLA), and custom pricing for the Enterprise plan with unlimited connectors and bespoke integrations. Prices are monthly and include updates and monitoring.

Which business systems can an MCP server connect to?

Italian ERPs and management systems (Zucchetti, TeamSystem, SAP), CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho), document archives, SQL databases, email and internal tools via REST APIs. PugliAI builds the connectors needed for your use case and keeps them up to date.

What is the difference between MCP hosting and autonomous AI agents?

MCP hosting is the infrastructure that connects AI to your data and tools; autonomous AI agents are the systems that use that infrastructure to reason and execute tasks. The MCP server is the secure bridge to your systems, the agent is what crosses that bridge to complete a task. They work well together, but can also be adopted separately.
