# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Italian SMB (PMI) decision-makers: CEOs, owners, and general managers of companies of roughly 10-250 employees, evaluating their first serious AI investment (€15K+ engagements). They arrive from search or LinkedIn, are often not deeply technical, and are comparing a handful of consultancies. Secondary audiences: CTOs and IT managers validating technical credibility, and English-speaking prospects on the /en/ mirror.

## Product Purpose
Lead generation for PugliAI, an Italian AI consulting firm offering AI infrastructure, AI agents, strategic consulting, and industry-specific solutions for PMI. The site converts visitors into contact requests, accelerator applications, and guide downloads. Success means qualified PMI leads who trust PugliAI with a five-figure engagement.

## Brand Personality
Premium and institutional. Authoritative, trustworthy, established: an executive-facing consulting firm, not a startup. Confidence is carried by specifics (ROI figures, certifications, case studies) rather than hype. Italian-first, grounded in the Italian business context, with full English parity.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-startup SaaS: purple gradients, identical feature-card grids, stock 3D blobs, buzzword copy.
- Big-4 corporate consulting: sterile handshake stock photos, bland blue, whitepaper walls of text.
- Cheap agency template: carousels everywhere, animated counters, icon soup.
- Crypto/tech-bro hype: neon glows, dark-mode-for-coolness, aggressive FOMO claims.

## Design Principles
1. **Authority through evidence.** Specific numbers, certifications, and case studies do the persuading; the design frames them and never shouts.
2. **Executive legibility.** A 55-year-old CEO on a phone reads everything comfortably: high contrast, generous type sizes, unambiguous hierarchy.
3. **Premium restraint.** Gold is an accent earned by importance, not decoration. Whitespace and typography signal the price point.
4. **Italian-first parity.** Every surface ships in both languages with equal craft; neither version is a second-class translation.
5. **Conversion without pressure.** A clear path to contact from every page, with no dark patterns or manufactured urgency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA target. The audience skews older and less technical: minimum 16px body text, ≥4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation, 44×44px touch targets. GDPR-compliant forms and consent flows.
