## Product & Purpose

PugliAI is an Italian AI-consulting company that helps small and medium enterprises
(PMI) adopt frontier AI **practically, compliantly, and with measurable ROI**. The site's
job is to win qualified inquiries: communicate credibility, the olive-tree brand story,
concrete packages and outcomes, then drive a "Get a quote / Richiedi un preventivo" action.

Core offering: all-inclusive AI packages from €15K (pre-built automations, strategic
consulting, team training); on-premise products (VoiceAI, KnowledgeAI Enterprise); a
startup Accelerator; delivery from proof-of-concept to scale in **90 days**, with ROI
written into the contract.

## Users & Purpose

- **Who:** owners, CEOs/founders, and operations/IT decision-makers at Italian SMEs;
  plus AI-driven startup founders (Accelerator).
- **Context:** evaluating whether AI is real, safe (GDPR / EU AI Act), and worth the
  spend; skeptical of abandoned pilots and hype.
- **Job to be done:** find a partner who delivers concrete, compliant, measurable AI fast
  — then request a quote / book a consultation.
- **Languages:** Italian (primary, root URLs) and English (`/en/`). Italian phone (+39),
  EUR pricing, CET/CEST, Italian business conventions.

## Brand Personality

- **Rooted + frontier.** "Radici nel territorio. Intelligenza nel futuro." Made-in-Italy
  warmth and trust, paired with serious, modern AI capability.
- **Concrete, not hypey.** Numbers, timelines, guarantees ("150% ROI", "90 days",
  "2–4 weeks"). Outcomes, not slideware.
- **Editorial + premium.** Dark, cinematic, confident; a literary serif accent against a
  clean grotesk. The living olive-tree canvas is the signature.
- **Trustworthy / compliant.** Digital sovereignty, on-premise, GDPR & AI Act "by design".

## Anti-references (what to avoid)

- The legacy PugliAI look (navy `#0A1628` + gold `#D4A017`, Inter everywhere) — fully retired.
- Generic AI-startup clichés: purple→blue gradients, Inter-for-everything, cards-in-cards,
  stocky "robot/AI brain" imagery, saturated cyan accents.
- Vague enterprise filler and unsubstantiated claims; keep copy specific and earned.

## Accessibility & Quality

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1 (watch muted text on the dark `--notte`
  and on the light `--paper` sections).
- Keyboard reachable; visible focus; skip-link present; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Performance targets: FCP < 1.5s, LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, TTI < 3.5s. Static, vanilla —
  no runtime framework; keep canvas animations to ≤ 2 per page.

## Strategic Design Principles

1. **Every page earns the inquiry** — clear path to "Get a quote" without being pushy.
2. **Dark editorial rhythm** — dark product sections punctuated by light explainer/proof
   sections; the olive-tree metaphor threads through.
3. **Proof over promises** — lead with numbers, guarantees, compliance, real outcomes.
4. **Bilingual parity** — IT and EN must stay in sync in content and quality.
5. **Consistency via the system** — reuse `DESIGN.md` components; don't reinvent per page.
